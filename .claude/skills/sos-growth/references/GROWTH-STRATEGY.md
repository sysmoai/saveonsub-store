# SaveOnSub — Evidence-first acquisition and SEO strategy

## Commercial objective
Recover and expand qualified Bangladesh subscription inquiries, verified paid orders, legitimate fulfilled purchases, retention and measurable contribution margin. Do not equate clicks with sales; do not guarantee AI citations, ranking or revenue. Protect the existing site before expanding it.

## Customer intent taxonomy
Research with GSC, site analytics, reputable search research and anonymized approved inquiry data:
- Transactional: [tool] subscription Bangladesh, [tool] price BD, premium [tool] bKash, monthly vs yearly, customer-owned/personal, activation, renewal.
- Comparative: ChatGPT vs Claude/Gemini/Grok, Perplexity free vs Pro, coding/image/video/voice/music tools, free vs premium, plan and eligibility comparisons.
- Job-oriented: research, student study, writing, business marketing, video generation, programming, productivity, design and automation.
- Trust and assistance: legitimate access method, provider policy, BDT payment, safe setup, refunds, renewals and data protection.
- Language: English, meaningful Bangla and romanized Bangla variations observed in real searches. Avoid thin duplicate landing pages or keyword stuffing.

Make a query-to-canonical map with source/date, intent, GSC data, existing target URL, competing existing URLs, approved offer status, content gap, priority, expected buyer outcome and review owner. Preserve established URLs. Prioritize actual impression/click opportunities and working commercial pages over speculative volume. Never merge other brands' data.

## Technical SEO and indexing
Audit www/apex, 200/301/404, redirects, canonical/hreflang EN-BN reciprocity, robots.txt, sitemap, accidental noindex, pagination, internal linking, crawlable static page content, truthful structured data, valid lastmod, mobile accessibility and Core Web Vitals. Compare source, staged output, CI and real canonical live pages; investigate divergence. Protect ranking URL equity. Validate generated JSON-LD against actual visible approved offers; held plans must not become orderable via schema. Do not falsely advance freshness.

## AI search discoverability / AEO-GEO
Write independent original helpful guidance that assistants may cite: accurate FAQs and comparisons, transparent access types, straightforward provider feature explanations with official source and verification date, clear business identity, meaningful headings and crawlable content. Genuine citations and mentions are useful; fake authority, hidden prompt instructions, doorway pages and programmatic thin content are prohibited. Measure AI referral sources where available and check a consistent, dated set of relevant user prompts. No promise that all assistants recommend this business. No magical llms.txt requirement or Google AI ranking loophole.

## Conversion
Instrument (only with legal/consent-safe, privacy-conscious events) product_view, plan_choice, WhatsApp_click, inquiry_received, qualified_lead, verified_payment, order_fulfilled and renewal as distinct events. Do not invent CRM records. Test mobile navigation, product filtering, accurate plan/price/access/availability, WhatsApp message content, confirmation-before-payment, order state, safety disclosures and EN/BN language consistency. Clarify shared/managed/personal access honestly. Improve meaningful trust elements, not fake reviews, invented orders, countdowns, guarantees or lowest-price claims. Prioritize high-intent funnels with measured drop-off; evaluate small cohorts to limit SEO regressions.

## Original content program
Create useful Bangladesh-specific pages about product suitability, evidence-backed comparisons, official plan limits, real payment/setup questions, troubleshooting and verified capabilities. Never publish unsupported resale eligibility. Use accurate provider citations and dates; do not scrape and repackage thin descriptions. Build internal links to the most relevant existing category/product/canonical pages.

## Prioritization
First secure security, provider eligibility, price/claims truth, release provenance and working conversion paths. Then analytics, indexation and high-intent content improvements. Then additional original content, authority and automation. Score tasks by verified business impact, confidence, effort, reversibility, blocking dependencies and risk. Each completed task must have a before-state, code diff, test results, approval status and measurable follow-up criterion.
