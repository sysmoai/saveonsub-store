## Economic decision and stopping rule
pre-ad contribution = settled revenue - actual provider/supplier landed cost - payment/FX fees - variable support/fulfillment - refund/fraud reserve.
after-ad contribution = pre-ad contribution - attributed CAC. 
If CEO requires at least BDT 500 net contribution **after ads**, maximum affordable CAC = verified pre-ad contribution - 500 BDT. Negative max CAC ⇒ DO NOT run paid acquisition for that SKU under this rule. The founder strategy document says this floor needs confirmation for SaveOnSub.
- Qualified lead rate = qualifying A leads / unique meaningful conversations (report unknown/duplicates).
- Paid conversion rate = verified paid customers / unique meaningful conversations.
- CAC = actual attributed spend / verified NEW paid customers; undefined until denominator > 0.
- Optimize primarily by post-ad contribution / new paid order / quality; do not reward cheap chats on their own.
- Stop when total CEO cap reached, economics worse than approved limit with enough observations, offer rights invalidated, delivery promises fail, complaint/refund threshold breached, or mismatched/duplicate Purchase signals.
- Avoid treating an anecdotal first 46-message dataset as a sufficient lookalike seed; validate its quality and consent first.
