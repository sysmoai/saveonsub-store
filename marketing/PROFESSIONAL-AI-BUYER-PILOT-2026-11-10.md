## Qualified buyer definitions
- High fit (A): concrete business/freelancing/coding/research workflow; named paid tool and plan/access requirement; pricing accepted; able to buy within seven days. No sensitive inferences.
- Potential (B): clear need and tool, but offer/budget/timing undecided; follow up with permission.
- Learning-only (C): general curiosity without current need or active decision; share an educational guide, not a hard sales push.
- Invalid (D): bot, unrelated, duplication, prohibited fulfillment request. Mark and exclude from sales numerator.
- Record only minimal lead alias, campaign/creative ID, source, desired SKU, use case, budget band as voluntarily stated, stage, amount invoiced/paid, source evidence and consent state; no raw message transcript or password.

## Measurement map & weekly decision
| Stage | Count/evidence | Leading owner |
|---|---|---|
| Reach, impressions, CPM | Meta account actual reporting | Ads Manager |
| Messaging conversations | Meta + unique conversation ID | Ads Manager / Ripon |
| Qualified A or B | timestamped CRM record | Ripon |
| Approved quote | approved offer + duration / access | SaveOnSub CEO or delegated approver |
| Verified paid order | merchant settlement and order ID | Human finance verifier |
| Contribution before ads | settled revenue - actual supplier cost - variable fees/support - refund reserve | SaveOnSub finance |
| Contribution after ads | prior amount - attributable ad spend | SaveOnSub finance |
| Repeat customer | separately verified renewal | Ripon + finance |

- Core diagnostic: **Qualified lead rate = qualified leads / unique inbound conversations**.
- Core business metric: **CAC = attributable ad spend / distinct confirmed new paid customers**. Undefined if paid count = 0.
- Protected contribution floor (if CEO confirms ৳500 after ads per order): **max sustainable CAC = verified pre-ad contribution - ৳500**. Negative/zero ⇒ no paid acquisition to that SKU under floor.
- ROAS alone is insufficient because reseller cost of goods sold can be high. Track cost/paid order and contribution net of acquisition.
- Weekly founder review: source by campaign/ad, creative-level qualified rate, verified purchases, product-level real margin, support load, refund risk, comments/objections. Mark missing data **UNKNOWN**, never zero.
- Stop/pause triggers: total budget cap reached, negative verified incremental contribution, fulfillment/access claims fail, tracking duplicates, provider-policy issue, or staff cannot maintain timely support. Scale only after multiple genuine verified orders with positive contribution and lead quality consistently observed.
