**PUBLIC SANITISED DRAFT / NOT AN ACTIVE SALES OR REFUND POLICY / DO NOT DEPLOY WITHOUT OWNER REVIEW**

## Immediate operating instruction, no new spend
- Continue existing customers' documented support and rights. Existing sales create responsibilities; a new website draft cannot retroactively take those away.
- Hold **new sales or paid activations of personal ChatGPT credentials shared with multiple unrelated customers**. Per [OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/) individual account credentials may not be shared with others.
- For anything else involving provider accounts: research first, quote only after CEO-approved product entitlement, allowed service model, exact customer ownership, full landed costs and clear limitations. An old draft poster, GitHub catalog entry or discounted price is NOT a sale authorization.
- Priority: settled payments and delayed service, then existing opted-in renewals, then compliant inquiry and onboarding services, then net-contribution evidence and SEO.
- Do not merge, redeploy, generate new mass posters or move prices merely from this document. Preserve existing useful SEO URLs and brand lock.

## Draft public replacement language, NOT LEGAL APPROVAL
**ChatGPT Plus page:** "Need ChatGPT Plus? We can explain official subscription options and, where permitted, help you set up your own account. Provider subscription charges and our optional setup/support charges are disclosed before ordering. We do not provide shared personal-account login access."

**Google AI Pro page:** "Our team may help you understand and set up an eligible Google AI subscription on your own Google account. Provider charges, regional eligibility, plan features and any separate assistance fee are confirmed before payment. Promotional/student entitlements are not transferable unless officially permitted."

**Delivery disclosure:** "Expected delivery or service completion depends on the exact service and current team availability. We confirm the estimated time and any written support conditions before you pay."

**Refund disclosure:** "Support and refund requests are reviewed against the written terms applicable to the original order and applicable law. Previously granted and statutory customer rights remain unaffected by prospective policy revisions."

**Payment disclosure:** "Use only the payment destination provided during verified order confirmation. We mark payment received only after our approved payment records confirm settlement; a screenshot alone does not prove a paid order."

**Account safety:** "Keep your provider account and password under your control. Do not send passwords, OTP codes, recovery keys or private customer chats to the sales team."

These are suggested copy directions, NOT changes to existing commercial contracts, actual prices, consumer rights or order promises. Emon approves final policy and wording after a legal/service review.
