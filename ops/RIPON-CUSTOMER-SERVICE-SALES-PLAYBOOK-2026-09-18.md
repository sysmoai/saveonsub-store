## 6. Shared vs personal
Do not assume every product can be shared.

- Personal/customer-specific: best for privacy, professional use, history/data continuity and reliable ownership/control.
- Team/Workspace/Family/Extra Member: sell only if the provider supports the arrangement for that product/use.
- Shared credentials: provider rules and privacy/continuity can make this unsuitable or prohibited. Do not sell it as a normal approved plan unless Emon has approved that exact method.

## 8. Customer conversation flow
### First reply
“Assalamu Alaikum. অবশ্যই সাহায্য করছি। আপনি কোন কাজের জন্য subscriptionটি নিতে চাচ্ছেন এবং personal account চান নাকি available lower-cost supported option জানতে চান?”

### After understanding need
Explain 1–2 best-fit options, not a confusing long list.

### Before payment
Send:
- exact product/tier;
- access type;
- duration;
- final price;
- activation expectation;
- important provider rule;
- payment number/method;
- short refund/service-assurance note.

### Payment
Verify transaction in the actual payment app. Screenshot alone is not enough.

### After activation
Confirm the plan is active and send expiry/renewal date + support instructions.

## 11. Refund/replacement message rule
Never say “refund guaranteed” or “1-hour replacement guaranteed” unless the customer's exact order terms say that.

Explain:
- SaveOnSub stands behind its own activation/fulfillment promise;
- provider controls its platform/features/policies;
- successful digital activation is normally not refundable for simple change of mind;
- if SaveOnSub makes a fulfillment error, we correct it or apply the order's remedy;
- provider/customer-caused issues are handled according to the provider rules and the written SaveOnSub policy.
