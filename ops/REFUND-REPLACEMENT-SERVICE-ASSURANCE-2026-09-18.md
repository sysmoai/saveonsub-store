# SaveOnSub Refund, Replacement & Service Assurance Policy
**Revision:** 2026-09-18  
**Applies to:** SaveOnSub subscription activation/payment-assistance orders  
**Status:** Controlling operational policy unless a product/order has more specific written terms or mandatory law gives the customer greater rights.

## 1. Responsibility split
SaveOnSub is an independent subscription/payment-assistance business and is not the underlying product provider unless explicitly stated.

### Provider controls
The underlying provider controls, among other things:
- platform uptime;
- features/models/limits;
- account enforcement;
- provider billing/refund rules;
- region/device/account eligibility;
- provider-plan changes;
- provider support decisions.

Customers must comply with the provider's current terms and policies.

### SaveOnSub controls
SaveOnSub remains responsible for what SaveOnSub itself promises and performs, including:
- correct order recording;
- correct plan/amount quoted under the approved process;
- payment verification;
- activation/fulfillment using the disclosed method;
- SaveOnSub-controlled seat/admin actions where applicable;
- the written service-assurance commitments attached to the order.

This policy does not attempt to remove rights that cannot legally be excluded.

## 2. Core principle
For digital subscriptions, once the correct paid access has been successfully activated/delivered and the customer can use it, the order is generally **not refundable merely because the customer changes their mind, stops using it, dislikes a provider feature, or finds another price later**.

Any mandatory legal right and the underlying provider's applicable refund entitlement still applies.

## 3. Full-refund situations
A full refund is normally due when:
- customer cancels before SaveOnSub has committed/purchased/activated the order and no irrecoverable provider cost has been incurred;
- SaveOnSub received payment but cannot supply the agreed product/access within the written order-specific delivery commitment and the customer does not accept a revised delivery time/alternative;
- SaveOnSub activated the wrong plan/account through SaveOnSub error and cannot correct it;
- duplicate payment is verified;
- SaveOnSub explicitly approved a refund in writing.

Refund eligibility is assessed using the order record, payment evidence and provider state.

## 4. Replacement / re-activation first where appropriate
When the problem is within SaveOnSub's control and can be corrected without reducing the purchased value, SaveOnSub may first:
1. restore access;
2. correct the activation;
3. re-activate;
4. replace a provider-supported seat/access unit with an equivalent one.

If a valid SaveOnSub-controlled issue cannot be restored within a reasonable order-specific resolution period, the customer may receive the remedy stated in the order terms, which can include a refund or a fair unused-period adjustment where appropriate.

There is **no universal 1-hour replacement promise** unless the specific order expressly states one.

## 5. Provider-side issues
After correct delivery, a provider outage, model/feature change, rate limit, regional change, price change, policy enforcement or provider service decision is controlled by the provider.

SaveOnSub will provide reasonable assistance — such as checking the account, explaining the provider rule or helping the customer contact provider support — but does not guarantee the provider will refund, reverse enforcement, restore a removed feature or maintain a specific model.

If the provider itself grants a refund/credit relating to a SaveOnSub order, SaveOnSub will reconcile the customer remedy according to the order, provider outcome and applicable law.

## 6. Customer-caused / excluded situations
Unless mandatory law or a specific written order term requires otherwise, SaveOnSub does not owe a refund/replacement for loss caused by:
- violating provider terms;
- reselling/re-sharing access contrary to the approved method;
- changing credentials or admin settings in a way not permitted by the purchased access method;
- providing wrong account/email details and approving activation to them;
- customer-side device/network problems unrelated to the subscription;
- unauthorized chargeback after successful delivery;
- abuse, fraud, security compromise caused by customer conduct;
- failure to use the subscription after correct delivery;
- provider feature preference/change-of-mind after activation.

## 7. Multi-user/team/family access
Only provider-supported or otherwise specifically approved multi-user access may be sold under the normal workflow.

Before payment, the customer should be told:
- whether they receive their own invite/profile/account or shared credentials;
- any admin/household/team restrictions;
- privacy/continuity implications;
- specific service-assurance period/terms.

If a provider-supported seat is removed because of a SaveOnSub-controlled admin/sourcing error during the written coverage period, SaveOnSub should restore/replace it where possible. If restoration is impossible, apply the order-specific refund/unused-period remedy.

Credential-sharing that conflicts with provider rules must not be disguised as provider-supported team/family access.

## 8. Refund request process
Customer sends to **WhatsApp +8801305869242**:
- order ID;
- payment TxnID/reference;
- problem description;
- screenshot/screen recording if relevant.

Internal process:
1. acknowledge the complaint promptly;
2. classify SaveOnSub / provider / customer cause;
3. verify payment and delivery evidence;
4. state next step or request missing evidence;
5. keep the customer updated;
6. document final resolution.

Operational goal: provide an initial substantive response and complaint-status update within **72 hours at the latest**, usually much sooner through the human support channel.

## 9. Refund payment control
- Refund to the original payer/payment route where reasonably possible.
- Never refund to an unrelated third party merely on chat instruction without verification.
- Record refund amount, reason, approval, transaction reference and timestamp.
- Approved refunds should be processed promptly; where third-party/provider processing is involved, timing may depend on that system.
- Do not promise "same day" universally unless the specific case can actually be completed that day.

## 10. Fraud / dispute control
If there is suspected fraud, conflicting payer identity, fake TxnID, unauthorized-payment allegation or chargeback:
- pause undelivered fulfillment;
- preserve order/payment evidence;
- verify the payer and payment route;
- escalate to Emon;
- cooperate with the payment provider as required.

## 11. Customer acceptance
Before payment, the customer should receive a short summary of:
- exact plan/access method;
- price/duration;
- provider rules;
- delivery expectation;
- refund/replacement/service-assurance terms.

Payment after that disclosure is evidence that the customer received the order terms, but it does not waive mandatory legal rights.

## 12. Language to use
Use **“SaveOnSub Service Assurance”** for SaveOnSub's own fulfillment commitment. Do not call it “provider warranty” unless that warranty actually comes from the provider.

## 13. Policy priority
If there is a conflict:
1. mandatory applicable law;
2. specific written terms attached to the customer's order;
3. this policy;
4. general/older website copy.

Older promises such as blanket 7-day/30-day warranties, universal one-hour replacement, or same-day refund do not control a new order unless they are expressly included in that order's current written terms.
