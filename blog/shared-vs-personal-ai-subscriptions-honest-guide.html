Shared vs personal subscriptions — the guide resellers don't write

Many sellers in Bangladesh advertise a very low price without saying what you are actually buying. The price difference almost always comes from one thing: whether the account is shared or yours alone. Here is the plain trade-off.

The trade-off

Shared planPersonal plan
PriceLower: several people split one subscriptionHigher: one subscription, one person
Who uses the accountSeveral peopleOnly you
Privacy of chats, files and historyOthers may see your activityOnly you see it
Provider termsMany providers say an account is for one person; check the provider's termsNormal individual use
ContinuityThe provider can reset or close a shared accountTied to your own login

How to choose

A shared plan can suit you when you only want to try a tool, your use is light, and nothing you put in it is private. Pick a personal plan when it is for work, client files, private chats, certificates, or you need it to keep working without interruption. Pick nothing when the free tier is enough: ask us on WhatsApp and we will say so.

FAQ

What is the difference between a shared and a personal subscription?

On a shared plan several people use one account and split the cost. On a personal (customer-specific) plan the account is yours alone. Every SaveOnSub plan is labelled with its access type before you pay.

Will other people see my data on a shared plan?

They can. On a shared account other users may be able to see chat history, files, settings or watch history, depending on the product. Do not put private, work or client data in a shared account.

Do providers allow shared accounts?

Many providers' terms say an account is for one person, and a provider can reset or close a shared account. Check the provider's own terms on its official site; choose a personal plan when continuity matters.
